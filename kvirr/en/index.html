Kvirr!

Bird quiz, guide and log

SupportPrivacy

Kvirr! is a free bird app for iPhone and Android: log the birds you see, learn 262 bird species (251 in Scandinavia and 199 in Britain), play the daily quiz and compare with friends. No account, no ads and no tracking. See also the privacy policy.

Contact

Need help, found a bug or have a suggestion? Email zonio.apps@protonmail.com. Please include your phone model and app version (see below). You usually get a reply within a few days.

FAQ

Which version do I have?

Open the menu (⋯) at the top of “Log”. The version and build number are shown there.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in. Your log, places and photos stay on your phone.

What is stored about me on the server?

Only what you share in “Friends”, and it is anonymous. You are signed in with a random user ID, without a name, email, phone number or password. Your friends see a nickname you choose yourself, not your real name, along with the bird you picked and your numbers from the daily challenge. See the privacy policy.

How do I add a friend?

Open the "Friends" tab and create a profile. Share your friend code (KV-…) or QR code, or scan or type your friend's code. Both of you must accept before you see each other. The app needs camera access only to scan QR codes.

Someone is sending me unpleasant requests. What can I do?

Open the request or the friend’s page in “Friends” and choose Block. The friend or request disappears, and you get no more requests or nudges from that person. Choose Report to email us about it, and we delete the account if it breaks the rules. You can also tap “Make a new friend code”, so nobody with the old code can find you.

The QR code doesn't work

Check that the app may use the camera (phone Settings › Kvirr!). Hold the code steady and well lit in the frame. If it still fails, type the friend code by hand.

Why does the app want my location?

Only to save where you saw a bird and to show species that are common near you. You can also pick a place on the map and keep location off. The location on your sightings stays on your phone. For "nearby species", an area of about 45 km is sent to GBIF, with nothing linking it to you. See the privacy policy.

Notifications don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Kvirr! in your phone settings and not turned off in the app's Settings. Notifications are scheduled on the phone; there is no notification server.

How do I back up my log?

Use the menu (⋯) at the top left of "Logg" (the log): export as a spreadsheet (CSV) or make a backup with photos (zip). The same menu has "Import from file", which also accepts a backup from Kvirr on iPhone and Android. New phone? Back up on the old one and import on the new one.

How do I delete my data?

  • The log: swipe a sighting, or tap "Delete sighting" inside it. Deleting the app removes the log too, so make a backup first if you want to keep it.
  • The friends account: Friends › your profile › "Delete friends account". This deletes your profile, friendships, friend code and the anonymous user.
  • Lost your phone? Email your friend code (KV-…) or your nickname and one of your friends, and we delete the account within 30 days.

Where do photos and sounds come from?

Wikimedia Commons and xeno-canto. Photographer, recordist and licence are shown with each photo and sound, with a link to the original. Species occurrence data comes from GBIF.
