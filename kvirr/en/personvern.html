Kvirr!

Bird quiz, guide and log

SupportPrivacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Kvirr is a bird app made by Hege Lundstad, who is responsible for the data described here. For questions, or to have something deleted, email zonio.apps@protonmail.com.

In short

Your bird log, the places you saw birds and your photos stay only on your phone. We have no access to them. The only thing sent to a server is what you choose to share in the "Friends" tab.

Friends

When you turn on Friends, this is stored with Google Firebase in Europe:

  • a random, anonymous user ID (no email, phone number or password)
  • the nickname and bird you chose
  • your friend code
  • your streak and kvitter from the daily challenge, which days this week you took it, and when they were last updated
  • how many species you have seen this year, if you choose to share it
  • which other anonymous user IDs you are connected to as friends, and requests and nudges between you (with the sender’s nickname)

Only your friends see your nickname, bird and numbers. Others can only send you a request if they have your friend code. Please use a nickname rather than your full name.

None of this is linked to who you are: there are no names, emails or phone numbers, only random IDs and the nicknames you have chosen. We do not look at this data, except when you ask us to delete an account or someone reports abuse.

We use this data only to make the friends feature work, because you asked for it. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except Google, which stores it for us under Google's data processing terms. Google may process the technical sign-in outside the EU, with the safeguards those terms provide.

Blocking and reporting

If you block someone in “Friends”, that is stored only on your phone. If you report someone, an email to us opens with the nickname and anonymous user ID of the person you report. We use it only to review and, if needed, delete the account, and delete the email once the case is settled.

Deletion

  • In the app: Friends › your profile › "Delete friends account". This deletes your profile, friendships, friend code, the requests and nudges you have sent, and the anonymous user.
  • Without the app, for example if you lost your phone: email zonio.apps@protonmail.com with your friend code (KV-…) or your nickname and one of your friends. We delete the account within 30 days.

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, and to complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Other things the app fetches

  • Photos and sounds are downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and xeno-canto. They see your IP address, as any website does.
  • Nearby species: The app asks GBIF (Global Biodiversity Information Facility) which birds have been seen in an area around you. It sends an area about 45 km across with you roughly in the middle, but nothing that links it to you.
  • Chance here now: When you open a species, the app asks GBIF how often it is reported around your home place or where you last logged a sighting (or where you are, if you tap “Use my location”). Again only an area about 45 km across (up to about 130 km where there are few records) is sent, and the location is not stored.
  • The location of your sightings stays on the phone and is used to show your log on a map.
